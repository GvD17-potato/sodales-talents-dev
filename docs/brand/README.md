## Authority

For visual and branding decisions:

1. The Sodales Talents SDD remains authoritative for Talents-specific requirements.
2. The Canva Sodales Brand Identity Guidelines are the authoritative corporate
   brand reference.
3. Project-specific visual and motion enhancements may supplement these sources
   but must not contradict them.
4. External reference websites and screenshots are inspiration only.

## Known Talents-specific requirements from the SDD

The Talents SDD already explicitly requires:

- Obsidian: `#111111`
- Soft Ivory: `#F4F2ED`
- Graphite: `#35373B`
- Electric Violet: `#5E4FB3`
- Accessible violet on dark surfaces: `#887BD8`
- Supporting Talents tones:
  - `#DAD4F5`
  - `#8072D2`
  - `#2A2440`
- Manrope for expressive/display typography
- Inter for UI/body typography
- flat, precise surfaces
- restrained borders
- minimal shadows
- official BrandWordmark usage
- no invented talent portraits
- Electric Violet used sparingly

Do not infer additional brand rules that are not visible in either the SDD or
the Canva brand guidelines.
